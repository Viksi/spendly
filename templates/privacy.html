Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records you log — amount, category, date, and description. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate your account, display your expense history and spending summaries, and keep the Service secure and functioning correctly. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functions like hosting, infrastructure, or email delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
